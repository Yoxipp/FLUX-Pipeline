# Code of Conduct

FLUX adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and to anyone
representing the project in public.

In short:

* Be welcoming and patient. Many people here are students or first-time contributors.
* Be respectful of different experience levels, opinions and backgrounds. Criticise ideas and code, never people.
* No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or imagery, and no publishing of someone's private
  information without their permission.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers through
**Security > Report a vulnerability** on the repository page (the only private channel GitHub offers), with
"Code of Conduct" in the title. Every report is reviewed and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
